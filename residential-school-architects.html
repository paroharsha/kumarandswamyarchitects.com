Residential & boarding school architects.

A boarding school is a small town: classrooms, hostels, dining, staff housing, sport and a day that runs from morning study to lights out. We have designed residential schools for more than twenty years, for schools teaching the IB, the Canadian curriculum and others, on campuses of up to 50 acres.
