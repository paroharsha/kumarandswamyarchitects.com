Education & campus · Bangalore

School architects in Bangalore.

Kumar & Swamy Architects has designed schools and educational campuses from Bangalore since the early 1990s, starting with Mallya Aditi International School. Across three generations the practice has built more than sixty institutions — 24 of them schools and colleges listed below — for day schools, residential schools and multi-institution campuses across India.

Scope

What we design.

Day & residential schools
K–12 schools for a few hundred to several thousand students, including boarding campuses with hostels and staff housing: Canadian International School, Amaatra Academy, The Cathedral Vidya School, Sri Kumarans Children’s Home (7,000 students).
Any curriculum
CBSE, IB, the Canadian curriculum and Montessori — the plan follows the way each school teaches: Valiants Academy (Montessori to grade 6, CBSE to grade 12), United World Academy and The Cathedral Vidya School (IB).
Campus masterplans
Multi-institution campuses planned in phases, such as the 50-acre PES Group of Institutions campus in Shimoga, with a K–12 school, an engineering college, hostels and an auditorium.
Extensions & revitalisation
New blocks and careful upgrades to working schools: a new extension to Mallya Aditi, thirty years after we designed it, and the campus-wide revitalisation of Good Shepherd International School, Ooty.
Climate-led design
Shade, cross-ventilation and local materials before machines: at Tejas International School in Bagalkote the interiors stay up to 4°C cooler than outside with no air conditioning.
Interiors, landscape & signage
Classrooms, libraries, landscape and wayfinding designed with the buildings, as at Wisdom Tree Global School, Bhubaneswar.

Questions

Planning a school?

Where do you work?

Our studio is in Cambridge Layout, Bengaluru. Most of our schools are in Bangalore and across Karnataka, and we have also built schools in Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh and Odisha.

Do you extend schools that are already running?

Yes. Extensions, new blocks and revitalisation make up a large part of our school work, including National Public School campuses, Mallya Aditi and Good Shepherd International School.

Who will we work with?

A family practice in its third generation, led by its partners — Sanchali Harsha, Paromita Harsha, C. S. Harsha, Suchitra Harsha. Interiors, landscape and signage can be designed by the same studio as the buildings.

How do we start?

Write to kumarswamy49@gmail.com or call +91 63624 28416. It helps to tell us the type of school, the location, the site area, how many students you plan for and your timeline.
