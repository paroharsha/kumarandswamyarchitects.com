## C. S. Harsha (Harsha Shivakumar), Partner

**Summary:** C. S. Harsha is a partner at Kumar & Swamy Architects in Bangalore, where he has led the practice's school and institutional work since 1990.

C. S. Harsha, also known as Harsha Shivakumar, was born and raised in Bangalore, the son of C R Shivakumar, who founded Kumar & Swamy in 1969. He completed his degree in architecture in 1990 **[? college]**. That same year, he and Sanchali Harsha, a fellow student, entered the design competition for Mallya Aditi International School and won it. It became the practice's first school, and the start of the educational work it is now known for.

Over the three decades since, Harsha has led the firm's work on schools and campuses across India **[? 3–5 named projects he led, and his role on each]**. In 2018 he and Sanchali returned to Mallya Aditi to design a lightweight steel first floor over the original load-bearing building, completed in 2021.

His approach starts from the child rather than the building: how children move through a school, where they gather, and how light and air reach every room. Mallya Aditi, organised around courtyards instead of corridors, set out that approach, and it runs through the practice's later schools. He has written about architecture and education in the firm's journal, most recently in *Learning Architecture*.

Outside the studio, Harsha is a patron of classical music and often hosts concerts at home. He is a keen motorcyclist who rides across South India, plays golf at the Karnataka Golf Association, and travels widely.
