50 Years of Kumar & Swamy

We celebrated our 50th anniversary with an Open Day, where we invited several of our old colleagues and friends who have been part of Kumar and Swamy's journey to see our office and our works.

We displayed some of the old, hand-drafted working drawings for projects like Mallya Aditi and Kanteerva stadium.

We also had on display some of CR Shivakumar's works and some of our new and more recent projects.

Many of our old associates came from all over Bangalore to wish us well. It was a day of remembrance and gratitude to CR Shivakumar and the legacy he left us. The story of his journey is the story of Kumar and Swamy.

Looking back at fifty years of architecture is overwhelming, and we feel deeply grateful to all those who came out to celebrate with us!
